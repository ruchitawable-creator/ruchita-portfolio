Featured Project

Disaster Management • AI-based concept

AI-Powered Disaster Resource & Evacuation Prioritization System

“Smarter Decisions. Faster Rescue. Saved Lives.”

An AI-based concept designed to help prioritize disaster-affected areas and support faster resource allocation during emergencies. The concept considers multiple factors to help disaster management teams identify areas that may need urgent attention.

  • Number of affected people
  • Severity of the disaster
  • Road accessibility
  • Distance from hospitals and shelters
  • Availability of resources

The main objective is to support better decision-making, prioritize critical areas, and help coordinate faster rescue operations.
